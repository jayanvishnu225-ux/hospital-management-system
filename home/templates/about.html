Serving Kochi since 2008

Built on trust, driven by care.

MediCare Hospital opened its doors in 2008 with a simple belief: that excellent medical care and genuine compassion belong together. Over the years, we've grown from a small clinic into a multi-specialty hospital serving thousands of families across Kerala.

Today, our 120+ specialists work across 12 departments — from cardiology and neurology to pediatrics and orthopedics — supported by modern diagnostics, advanced surgical suites, and a team that puts patients first at every step.
